# Admin webapp (all authenticated screens + sign-in + wizard)

Mode: Operate. Audience: home operator/admin, desktop and phone equally. Dark only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: ATLAS is a calm presence you can see: a living meridian globe is the companion, and every screen around it is a quiet celestial instrument. Refuses the default grey-card shadcn stack and the neon-glow AI dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Night-ink ground (blue-black, never pure black), stepped ink surfaces separated by hairline graticule rules, porcelain text, one ecliptic-gold accent for primary/active/live only. Meridian rings and graduated tick scales as the only ornament; geometry at fixed angles. Wide-set caps labels, mono numerals for every reading. No glow halos.

STORY: Operator opens ATLAS, sees it is alive and listening, sees recent turns and latency, then goes to configure without leaving the instrument.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left rail (globe mark + wordmark, grouped nav). Home: large meridian orb left/centre, status line under it, readouts (turns, median ms, wake attempts) right. Phone: orb top, readouts stacked, drawer nav.

FORM: Hybrid of user pick "Companion core" and grounded #4 "Celestial instrument / armillary"; seed 719112c1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
